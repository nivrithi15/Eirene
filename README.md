## About Eirene

**Eirene** is a digital care platform dedicated to postpartum women, combining **mental health support, recovery tracking, and personalized wellness guidance**.

The idea is simple:

> **While everyone asks how the baby is doing, Eirene asks: *How are you doing, Mom?***

Eirene focuses on the mother's physical and emotional recovery during the postpartum period and provides continuous, gentle support between clinical visits.

---

## The Problem

Postpartum care often focuses heavily on the newborn while the mother's own recovery receives significantly less continuous attention.

According to the research included in our proposal:

* **1 in 7 women** experience postpartum depression.
* In countries such as India, estimates can reach **22%+**.
* **50–80%** experience "baby blues" during the first weeks.
* More than **50% of cases remain undiagnosed**.

The problem is driven by:

* Mental-health stigma
* Attention shifting almost entirely toward the baby
* Lack of continuous postpartum care
* Limited monitoring between medical appointments

Existing digital health applications frequently focus on **infant tracking or pregnancy**, leaving a gap in continuous maternal mental-health and postpartum recovery support.

---

## Our Solution

Eirene is a **mother-first postpartum care platform** that provides:

### Emotional Health Support

* One-tap mood and energy check-ins
* Multi-day mood-pattern detection
* Context-aware AI companionship
* Personalized coping suggestions
* Safety protocols for distress signals

### Recovery Tracking

* Recovery progress dashboard
* Mood and energy trends
* Private journaling
* Photo-based recovery and milestone tracking

### Postpartum Wellness

* Gentle recovery-focused guidance
* Hydration and rest reminders
* Postpartum nutrition guidance

### Care Ecosystem

* Location-based verified doctor and specialist listings
* Support for transitioning from digital guidance to professional clinical care
* Personalized reminders based on individual patterns

These modules are designed to be **safe, private, supportive, and not overwhelming**.

## What Makes Eirene Different?

Most generic baby-focused applications prioritize:

**Baby → Feeding → Diapers → Sleep → Growth**

Eirene changes the perspective to:

**Mother → Mental Health → Recovery → Wellness → Support**

Our goal is to address what we call the **"Puerperium Gap"** — the critical postpartum period when maternal health needs continuous attention but is often insufficiently monitored.

* Certified doulas

This creates trusted community-driven adoption and word-of-mouth growth.
